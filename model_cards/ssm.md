# State-Space Models (SSM)

**Category:** Latent dynamical models  

## Constraint profile
- 🎯 Predictive: 🔥🔥🔥  
- ✂️ Minimality: 🔥🔥 (state compression)  
- ⏱️ Temporal: 🔥🔥🔥🔥  
- 👁️ Observation: 🔥🔥🔥  
- 🛡️ Invariance: ⚪  
- 🧩 Structure: 🔥🔥🔥  

## Intuition
SSMs model latent states evolving over time with explicit dynamics and observation models.

## CLSM interpretation
Strong temporal coherence and observation compatibility; structured but limited invariance handling.
