# VAE (Variational Autoencoder)

**Category:** Latent variable / reconstruction-based models  

## Constraint profile
- 🎯 Predictive: ⚪  
- ✂️ Minimality: 🔥🔥🔥 (via KL regularization)  
- ⏱️ Temporal: ⚪  
- 👁️ Observation: 🔥🔥🔥🔥  
- 🛡️ Invariance: ⚪  
- 🧩 Structure: 🔥🔥 (prior structure)  

## Intuition
VAEs learn latent representations that reconstruct observations while regularizing latent variables.

## CLSM interpretation
Strong observation compatibility and explicit pressure toward minimality; limited predictive and invariance properties.
