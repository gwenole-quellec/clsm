# SuStaIn (Subtype and Stage Inference)

**Category:** Domain-specific structured models  

## Constraint profile
- 🎯 Predictive: 🔥🔥 (prognostic use)  
- ✂️ Minimality: 🔥🔥 (low-dimensional staging)  
- ⏱️ Temporal: 🔥🔥🔥🔥 (progression constraints)  
- 👁️ Observation: 🔥🔥🔥  
- 🛡️ Invariance: ⚪  
- 🧩 Structure: 🔥🔥🔥🔥  

## Intuition
SuStaIn models disease progression with latent stages and subtypes derived from data.

## CLSM interpretation
Very strong structural and temporal constraints; interpretability prioritized over flexibility and invariance.
