# CPC (Contrastive Predictive Coding)

**Category:** Predictive representation learning  

## Constraints profile
- 🎯 Predictive: 🔥🔥🔥🔥
- ✂️ Minimality: 🔥🔥 (implicit)
- ⏱️ Temporal: 🔥🔥
- 👁️ Observation: ⚪
- 🛡️ Invariance: 🔥🔥 (partial)
- 🧩 Structure: ⚪

## Intuition
CPC learns representations optimized for predicting future observations via a contrastive objective, without reconstructing inputs.

## CLSM interpretation
Strong emphasis on predictive sufficiency with partial temporal coherence; weak grounding and structure.
